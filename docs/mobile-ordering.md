# Mobile ordering guide

Updated September 15, 2026.

- **Choose a store.** Open the store dropdown in the header and type in **Search stores**. Tap the matching name, or use arrow keys and Enter. Searching or pressing Escape leaves the current store unchanged. Choosing a store restores its working draft. If the latest visible notes could not be saved, the app keeps you in that store so you can retry, copy or export the notes first.

- **Add several flavors together.** Open a product and set each flavor's quantity with the − / + buttons or by typing. For example, set cranberry to 2 and apple to 2, then choose **Add selected flavors**. Leave unwanted flavors at 0. Search long flavor lists without losing selections. **Order unit** and the optional note apply to all selected flavors; each becomes a separate editable order line. One Undo removes the whole batch. Single-flavor products and scanned flavors start at 1. The batch uses the same automatic saving as other draft edits.

- **Create products from a picture (owner).** Choose **Catalog → Add from photo** or **Gemini → Add product from photo**. Take or upload a clear product photo, then choose **Analyze photo**. Review the detected name, flavor, barcode and case count. Choose **New product** or **Add a flavor to an existing product**, then **Review product** and **Save product**. Existing product details and pictures are kept when adding a flavor; you can explicitly choose to use the new picture. Prices are entered or confirmed in the editor, never inferred from a retail label. Unreadable fields stay blank. Photos support JPEG, PNG and WebP up to 5 MB. Reading or canceling a photo does not change the catalog. Adding a first named flavor to an existing standard product keeps its original Standard choice available for existing orders.

- **Browse and search.** Catalog opens in the compact **List** view. Choose **Grid** to set **Columns** from 1–5 and turn **Compact** on or off. Switching views preserves your grid settings; these choices belong to your account on this device. On a narrow phone screen, four- and five-column tiles open the full product name, options, price and quantity controls when tapped. Exact product names and codes rank first; searching `SS` puts the exact name ahead of related products and excludes incidental matches such as “floss.” The current-order bar opens your working draft for review. Its total is an estimate until submission. Mobile fields avoid unwanted focus zoom; pinch zoom remains available.

- **Appearance.** The Dispatch layout uses orange controls, warm ivory backgrounds, compact typography and the original Alabama Wholesale logo. Light is the default for a new account on a device; your explicit theme preference is retained. Use the sun icon to switch between light and dark.

- **Ask AI.** Select a store, then choose **Ask AI** in Catalog or Build. Type an order, dictate using your keyboard’s microphone, or attach a JPEG, PNG or WebP photo up to 5 MB. Review the proposed products, flavors, units and quantities before adding them. AI suggestions can be wrong or incomplete. This assistant prepares drafts; it does not submit or email an order for you.

- **Automatic draft saving.** Accepted edits normally start saving after 600 milliseconds without another edit, or within two seconds during continuous editing. These are scheduling intervals, not guaranteed server response times. **Saved online** appears only after the server confirms the current draft. Sign into the same account and select the same store on another device to restore its available online drafts. While the app is visible, it checks for draft updates about every 30 seconds; it postpones applying updates while a field or dialog is active, preserving your editing position.

- **Offline work and conflicts.** **Only on this device** means the draft has a device copy but its latest changes are not confirmed online. That copy cannot follow you to another device and may be lost if browser data is cleared or removed. **Not yet protected** means neither copy is confirmed: keep the tab open, reconnect or export your workspace. Online saving can continue when device storage is full. Concurrent edits are preserved for review instead of silently overwriting each other; export a copy before choosing which version to keep. Submission still needs a reliable device retry queue and server confirmation. A saved draft is not a submitted order.

- **All store orders.** Open **Orders → All stores** to see combined order history for every store your account can access. Each row shows the store name. Filter by status and use **Load older orders** to read earlier pages. **Selected store** returns to the store shown at the top of the app. Switching the history view keeps your ordering store and working draft unchanged.

- **Order emails.** Open an already submitted order and choose **Send / schedule email**. Its invoice PDF goes to the fixed recipient **alwholesaleorders@gmail.com**. Scheduling uses your device’s local date and time. Once the dedicated sender is connected and automatic emailing is enabled, newly submitted orders receive a five-minute delay, allowing you to change or cancel the pending email. Enabling this setting does not send existing orders in bulk. The scheduled time is the earliest time the queue may send; delays are possible, and provider acceptance does not prove inbox delivery. Email failure does not undo an order saved online. Sender setup is currently pending Google account verification, so sending controls remain unavailable until that connection is complete.

- **Capacity and setup limits.** The isolated test with 100 synthetic customers checks separate drafts, safe retries, access restrictions and conflicts. It is not a production load or response-time guarantee. Hosting is configured for up to five instances with 80 concurrent requests per instance. The minimum is zero warm instances, so the first request after an idle period can have a cold-start delay. These settings do not establish a supported user count. A personal Gmail sender also has a daily sending limit: Google warns of blocking after more than 500 emails in a day. [Google’s Gmail sending limits](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/22839?hl=en).

- **Shorter address options.** `alwholesaleorders.com`, `alwholesale.app` and `aworders.com` appeared available when checked on September 14, 2026. Availability can change. No domain was purchased or connected; the existing app address continues to work.
